# Three-minute judging demonstration

1. **Problem (20 seconds).** Decades of NASA combustion evidence are spread across investigations and files. Researchers need to find applicable evidence without treating unlike tests as equivalent.
2. **Discover (30 seconds).** Open Investigations. Search “smoke particle detection.” Show SAME and SAME-R, their match reasons and distinct coverage. Open the SAME report conclusion card and its page reference. Explain that topic points are not danger ratings.
3. **Compare (30 seconds).** Select SAME and FLEX. Compare smoke/aerosol detection with liquid-droplet suppression. Show the warning against numerical pooling of unlike fuel regimes.
4. **Real measurements (35 seconds).** Open Data Laboratory. Inspect Saffire condition points and S2-1 details. Select S2-5 and S2-6 to compare their spread rates, durations and different airflow conditions. Reported non-spreading silicone behavior applies only to the tested conditions. Enable the BASS-II CSV and inspect B2: retain 1.6, flag calibration, preserve the raw record.
5. **Evidence assistant (30 seconds).** Ask “Compare B1 and B4.” Follow the source. Without a provider key, explain that this is deterministic retrieval, not live LLM synthesis. In configured server mode, show the actual mode label and any fallback notice.
6. **Evidence gap (25 seconds).** Enter a lunar scenario with oxygen 30%, pressure 70 kPa and PMMA. Show relevant material investigations and explicit gravity/pressure/range gaps. Do not claim the scenario is safe or unsafe.
7. **Reproducibility (10 seconds).** Export JSON with units, citations, original CSV row locations and flags.

Closing line: “FlameWise makes combustion evidence easier to discover, compare and trace—while keeping its scientific limits visible.”
